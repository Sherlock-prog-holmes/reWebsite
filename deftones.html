WHY I LOVE DEFTONES

Deftones have a really special atmosphere. Their music feels heavy, dreamy and emotional at the same time. I love how they can make a song sound both aggressive and peaceful.

Their sound makes me feel like I'm somewhere else. It's the kind of music I can listen to when I want to disconnect from everything. Deftones is my favourite band. I can't really explain why, but their music takes me to another world. Whenever I listen to their songs, I feel like I'm completely escaping reality. Their atmosphere, sounds and the emotions they convey make me feel something I can't find in any other band.

THE VIBE

dark rooms / distorted guitars / nostalgia / dreamlike sounds

MY FAVOURITE THING

I love the contrast between the soft and heavy parts. That's what makes their music so unique to me.
